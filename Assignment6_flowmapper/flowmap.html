Flow Pattern Interpretation

“The most prominent areas of this pattern appear to be in the Southern/West areas of the U.S. which exhibit large node sizes that show a significant attraction…” High netflow regions act as major receivers of genealogical movement.

Flows generally move east-to-west and north-to-south, reflecting historical migration during 1887–1924. Smaller nodes represent areas that served as sources rather than destinations.

“High total flow (as seen by darker colors on the choropleth map) being aligned with higher node size…” This indicates that high-activity regions act as central hubs, while peripheral regions send more flows than they receive.
