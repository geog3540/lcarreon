About Me

My academic interests include LiDAR-based hydrology, ecological mapping, and geospatial data visualization. I enjoy working with ArcGIS Pro, Observable Plot, QGIS, Envi 5.7, and JavaScript-based mapping tools. Outside academics, I enjoy environmental fieldwork, gaining cultural education through travel, as well as hiking and being outdoors.
